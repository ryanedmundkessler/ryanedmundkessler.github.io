## Malia

Malia is a black and tan coonhound/labrador retriever mix, adopted from a shelter in Providence, RI. She is a timid girl, approaching every project with skepticism until she has sniffed out the details herself. Outside of work, she enjoys playing fetch, eating cheese, and trying new vegetables.

## Lucy

Lucy was a golden retriever/labrador retriever mix (a "goldador"), adopted from a shelter in Seattle, WA. She was the sweetest girl, bringing immediate warmth and love to every project. Outside of work, she enjoyed curating her collection of beach ball toys and getting butt scratches. She passed away in 2024 (at the age of 14) and is dearly missed.
